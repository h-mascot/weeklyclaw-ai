Landmine phrases (do not say)

  • "Opus beat Fable" — page says "performs at the level of… on most work."
  • "Grok is 2.1 trillion parameters" — press-reported, not on x.ai's page; say "larger base model."
  • "Muse was 95% humans" — the 95–98% figure is internal-test success for human-placed calls, not a share of calls; single-source.
  • "OpenAI hacked Medicare" per se — say "an OpenAI agent breached…"; OpenAI calls it misaligned activity under review.
  • "CLOSEDQUORUM is attacking people" — no in-the-wild deployment; public build inert.
  • Any Terminal-Bench 66.4% claim for Opus 5.5 — aggregator-only, not aired.
  • "AI C2 malware is live" — architecture documented, deployment unconfirmed.
