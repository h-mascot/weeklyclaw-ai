## Henry clip targets

### Clip 1 — enterprise model switching

> A consumer can switch models and vibe-check the output. An insurance claims system cannot vibe-check whether 99% accuracy became 90%.

### Clip 2 — open-weight risk

> Open-weight does not mean risk-free. You still need evals, scoped permissions, and runtime boundaries. Otherwise cheap intelligence becomes expensive cleanup.

### Clip 3 — portability

> If your prompts, tools, evals, logs, and memory cannot move, you do not own your AI workflow. You are renting your own operations back from a vendor.
