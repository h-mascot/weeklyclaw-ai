# Weekly Claw #21 — Host Cheat Sheet

## Hard constraints

- **Runtime:** 45 minutes. Stop at 44:00, outro by 45:00.
- **Recording:** tech check before record; cold open starts the file.
- **No abandoned segments:** if Tool Fight is teased, run it.
- **No live research:** if a claim is uncertain, label it and move.

## Episode title

**The model is becoming portable. The workflow is becoming the moat.**

## Cold-open lines to hit

1. “Last week was the price shock. This week is the ownership shock.”
2. “If intelligence is cheap, the question becomes who owns the workflow.”
3. “The model is becoming portable. Context is becoming capital.”

## Henry clip targets

### Clip 1 — enterprise model switching

> A consumer can switch models and vibe-check the output. An insurance claims system cannot vibe-check whether 99% accuracy became 90%.

### Clip 2 — open-weight risk

> Open-weight does not mean risk-free. You still need evals, scoped permissions, and runtime boundaries. Otherwise cheap intelligence becomes expensive cleanup.

### Clip 3 — portability

> If your prompts, tools, evals, logs, and memory cannot move, you do not own your AI workflow. You are renting your own operations back from a vendor.

## Andy moderation moves

Use these to prevent drift:

- “I’m going to compress that into one sentence…”

- “What would change your mind?”
- “Is that evidence, inference, or vibe?”
- “Let’s not live-research it; call it launch-week claims and move.”
