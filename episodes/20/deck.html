04 / HOT TAKES
Standing anchor · the part that travels on X

Two takes. Come argue.

TAKE ONE

The frontier-model moat is basically gone

Grok at $2, DeepSeek at $0.14, Cursor's CEO swapping daily drivers in an afternoon — the model isn't the moat. The moat moved up the stack: harness, routing, workflows, distribution.

Within a year, “which model” is a config setting most builders barely think about. Own the layer above the model.
TAKE TWO · the spicy one

Would you actually run DeepSeek in prod?

The cost math says yes; the politics say maybe not. Most builders quietly will — the price gap is too big to ignore for background work where the data isn't sensitive.

Prediction: first “company got burned running Chinese models” headline within a couple months — then everyone pretends they were always careful.

06 / SIGNAL FROM OUTSIDE
Signal from outside · the one video worth your time

Jensen Huang on open agent systems

NVIDIA's CEO with LangChain's Harrison Chase — “Why companies need open agent systems.” Sounds like an all-hands snoozer. It is not.

THE HARNESS

It's not the model

AI got useful when people stopped treating it as raw capability and wrapped it in a harness they own — tools, memory, domain context, the learning loop. That's agent frameworks. That's the game.

MODEL SELECTION

Start high, then specialize

Frontier model to find the ceiling; then specialized sub-agents for critical workflows — where cheap open-weight models shine because you fine-tune and self-host. When intelligence is cheap, you use more of it.

STRATEGY, NOT PROCUREMENT

Own your workflows

A company is “a collection of proprietary, super-important workflows” — whoever encodes those into agents on infra they control wins. He + Chase announce a Deep Agents + OpenShell reference architecture.

Why it matters: the CEO of a $3-trillion company is describing the open-harness, route-between-models, own-your-infrastructure playbook as the winning architecture. That's the consensus forming in real time.
