What you say when you do not know

"I do not know that yet. The on-screen label is what we verified."

Do not guess. Every card carries its own evidence label; if a host is asked for a number that is not on the card, the card is the answer.

On-air prohibitions

  • No claim of independent verification for Koa, Gemini's τ-Voice numbers, TypeSafe's ratios, or Atria's rankings.
  • No claim that MLPerf's best-submission deltas are hardware improvements.
  • No recap of the recap. It is delivered once, by Andy, in the close.
  • No reference to prior episode approval status; hosts do not discuss process on air.
  • Nothing autoplays anywhere in the deck.

If a link or asset fails on air

The card still has its headline, its numbers, and its evidence label. Say: "link is in the show notes." Move on. Do not stall the segment.
