04 / HOT TAKE · 3 MIN
Hot take · the fight · two sides, one verdict

AI sentience is a workplace question now.

The underlying sentience poll is unverified. This debate is different: what should operators do when the evidence is uncertain and the cost of being wrong may be large?

HENRY · SKEPTIC

It is autocomplete with a PR problem.

A viral percentage measures vibes, not experience. Treating it as evidence invites safety theater and pulls attention from failures we can measure.

  • The evidence: no agreed test for subjective experience
  • The risk: anthropomorphism hides ordinary product defects
  • Mind-changer: reproducible welfare-relevant behavior across models
ANDY · PREPARE ANYWAY

The hedge is cheap.

You do not need certainty to write a policy. If the expected moral weight is nonzero, treating the question seriously costs less than discovering too late that it mattered.

  • The precedent: major labs already fund AI-welfare work
  • The policy: document what would trigger a review
  • Mind-changer: strong evidence that the concern is operationally empty
The morally hard part is not the model. It is that we now have to take the poll seriously.
