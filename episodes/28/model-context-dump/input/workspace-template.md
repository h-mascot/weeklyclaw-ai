## Rules

- **What Happened This Week slides are visual prompts, not briefing documents.** Use one dominant picture, video, demo, chart, or live webpage per story. On-slide copy is limited to a headline plus zero to two short context lines. No body paragraphs, bullet lists, comparison cards, metadata panels, evidence blocks, or talking points. Put details, caveats, citations, numbers, and presenter guidance in speaker notes. Prefer playable video or a source artifact that opens live while remaining visible on the slide before the click.
- Henry's on-air material is concise talking points, an optional line, and a handoff cue. Never force a word-for-word Henry script.
- Andy's on-air material includes a complete fallback talk track he can use if he loses his place. It should sound spoken, not like documentation.
- General segment bullets may precede host material. Do not duplicate those bullets verbatim inside Andy's fallback prose.
- Do not force rigid alternating-speaker dialogue. Use the pattern that fits the segment: talking points → optional line → handoff cue, with Andy fallback prose where he participates.
- Hot Take / Debate must not repeat a news proposition, framing, or question.
- Integrate video research and its verified talk content directly into the relevant segment.
- If a video-research file lacks a usable talk-track, write one from verified evidence rather than assuming it exists.
- Use only research files relevant to the active episode/date. Verify primary claims and label vendor claims.
- Preserve a 32–38 minute target and 45-minute hard stop. Cut/compress the optional rotating block before cutting Signal From Outside.
