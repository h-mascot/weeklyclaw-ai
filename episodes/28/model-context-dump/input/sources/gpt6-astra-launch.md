- Benchmark figures are vendor-reported; ExploitBench 100% is an aggregate coverage score, not "hacked everything".
- Cyber "critical" designation applies to the Daybreak Blue configuration, not the default production model.
- Early access is limited (Daybreak orgs); general availability "in the coming days" — a Friday 4 PM ET show talks about a model most viewers cannot yet use.
