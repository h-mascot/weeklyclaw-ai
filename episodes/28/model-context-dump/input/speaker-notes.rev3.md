In Meta engineer comparisons: ~20% fewer tool calls and ~25% fewer tokens than 1.2; better adversarial robustness and calibration on irreversible actions. Open-weights release teased on the roadmap. Max reasoning mode "shortly" (pending safety testing) — so full-power benchmarks are not final. Third-party: Artificial Analysis release page (2 variants), DataCamp.
  - **C3 fal H3 Max (Sep 1):** post-trained MiniMax H3 video model — fal took the open H3 weights, added RL-based post-training for prompt adherence/visual quality, and co-designed the inference engine. Result: ~5s video in ~3s wall time (~35x official H3 endpoint throughput; ~15x faster than comparable-quality models). #1 on Design Arena image-to-video (Elo 1,341 vs official H3 at 1,333) and #1 on Artificial Analysis image-to-video-with-audio (Elo 1,201).
