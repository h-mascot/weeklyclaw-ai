## Grid B

### B1 NYC moratorium

- One-year student-facing generative-AI moratorium through grade 8.
- About 600K students; Reuters says roughly 40 tools, Chalkbeat says 38 contracts affected.
- No AI grading; high-school literacy modules and five supervised pilots remain.
- Henry’s concern: unequal access and the electricity analogy.

**Landing:** “The gap is the experiment.”

### B2 Visko Orbis 1.0

- First “Live Model”: living worlds streamed and steered in real time.
- Product distinction is world persistence, not another generated clip.
- $10M pre-seed reported.
- Closed source; launch reel/evaluations vendor-led.
- Demand latency, persistence, control, failure rate, and cost receipts.

**Landing:** “A good demo is not yet a medium.”

### B3 GPT-6 Astra launch

- Released Thursday 2026-09-03; announced in a press briefing (Axios, TechCrunch, The New Stack).
- Largest run ever: 100k+ GPUs at Stargate TX; first model with other models supervising training in a significant role.
- Brockman: “generational leap”, “Welcome to the AGI era” — his framing, attribute it.
- First model at the “critical” cybersecurity threshold; advanced cyber work gated to Daybreak trusted testers.
- $10/$50 per M tokens (matches Fable 5.1); Astra + Astra Pro only, no Luna/Terra/Sol variants.
- Caveats: vendor-reported benchmarks; ExploitBench 100% is an aggregate coverage score; OpenAI calls the monitorability decline serious.
