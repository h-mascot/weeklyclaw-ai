WHAT HAPPENED THIS WEEK · 1 / 5
What happened this week · the money/geopolitics beat

BIS rule controls China-designed chips. TSMC, Samsung suspend shipments.

Federal Register · Nov 19 · govinfo capture

The rule treats design, not just export.

FR Doc 2026-23529, published AND effective Nov 19: foundries and packagers must treat any advanced chip designed in China as a controlled AI chip unless the designer is US-approved — wherever the buyer is. The smartphone/non-AI-use exemption is gone; in-transit shipments are grandfathered to Dec 21.

▶ LIVE · federalregister.gov · FR Doc 2026-23529

Foundries and guidance moved the same morning.

FT (corroborated via wire syndication): TSMC and Samsung told Chinese clients certain chips will not ship while licenses pend — ByteDance, Baidu’s Kunlun unit, and Xiaomi/Oppo/Vivo consumer chips; China was ~5% of TSMC’s Q3 revenue. Nvidia confirmed it no longer ships licensed Hopper data-center chips to China. Reuters exclusive: Treasury and OSTP have discussed requiring US firms to disclose Chinese-model use — deliberation-stage, no rulemaking. Label it as exactly that on air.

Claude solves 2 black-hole problems; OpenAI agents finish 46% of research tasks.

anthropic.com capture · Nov 17

Claude closed two open black-hole problems.

It completed the calculation physicists set out in 2003 — proving the Hawking–Page black-hole phase transition is abrupt even where spacetime has no definite shape — and found the exact optimal modular-bootstrap ceiling, Λ≈9.1266. A self-organized group of Claude agents ran ~6 weeks; verified in Lean; every main result checked by a physicist. One method builds on an OpenAI model’s sphere-packing proof.

07 / HOT TAKE · 4 MIN
Hot take · the proposition

ANT is a public filer now. Quarterly guidance tests the safety brand.

Same receipt as slide 5 · reuse disclosed here · a cross-ideology coalition picked pricing day to argue the brand itself is the risk.

The proposition

Anthropic just sold roughly $79B of primary stock at $83 on a safety brand that Pinker, Pearl and three Fields Medalists publicly rejected the night before. Both sides of that sentence can be true: the brand can be fairly contested and the RSP discipline can still be real. What public markets will actually test is neither — they test whether “RSI as competitive advantage” survives its first guidance call, and whether quarterly pressure reprices safety from moat to cost center.

What would change Henry’s mind: Anthropic’s first earnings call keeping the safe-frontier-lab framing with RSI still booked as upside — or better, an RSP update filed as an 8-K. If safety only ever appears in marketing and footnotes, the signatories were right.

Henry

Every frontier lab is becoming a reporting company. The safety case is about to be a line item in a 10-Q. Watch which footnote disappears first.

Andy

The signatories are right that the brand was doing regulatory work. But going public cuts both ways — the S-1 already put the RSP threshold on the record. Disclosure is a governance tool too, and now it is mandatory.
