04 / HOT TAKE · 3 MIN
Hot take · 3 min · distinct from every news segment

Provenance is involvement, not authorship.

Anthropic's text watermark changes the source of randomness in low-stakes word choices. A positive detection means Claude likely processed some of the content — not that Claude authored it.

COVERAGE MAP

Where the watermark works.

Detection confidence grows with passage length and available interchangeable word choices. Exact facts, code, proofreading, and heavy edits provide little room for a mark.

  • Long prose: stronger signal
  • Translation: more markable
  • Facts + code + light edits: sparse signal
  • Short passages: too little entropy
PRODUCTION-LAYER FIX

Signed receipts, not watermark guesses.

The defensible position is to stop pretending detectors settle attribution. Start designing workflows where the agent's identity is a content-keyed, hash-chained, timestamped receipt — not a guess about which sampler touched it.

  • Involvement ≠ authorship
  • Detector API: coming soon
  • C2PA file credentials are a separate path
  • The model becomes the new plausible deniability

05 / CLOSE
One to watch + where to follow

The closing conditions

Stripe–OpenRouter is signed, not closed. If the deal closes before August 28, the gateway and wallet ship as one company and the neutrality promise becomes a one-quarter-old commitment under audit. If the close slips, the test shifts to whether the OpenRouter catalog and pricing stay stable through the regulatory tail.

The speed arms race

Cerebras CS-4 begins shipping this quarter. vLLM and llama.cpp DFlash 2 pull requests are open. When the upstream serving stacks merge the drafter, every operator's effective throughput jumps — and so does every operator's wall-clock budget for reasoning.

The detector API

Anthropic says the Claude detector is "coming soon." Until it ships, the watermark is a triage signal, not an authorship verdict. The production-layer fix is signed receipts — identity, authorization, timestamp — that do not depend on which sampler touched the text.
