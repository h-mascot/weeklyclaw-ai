Source 01 · OpenAI

Previewing GPT-5.6 Sol

OpenAI began a limited preview of GPT-5.6 models: Sol as the flagship, Terra as the balanced everyday model, and Luna as the fast lower-cost option.

https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/

Why it matters
  • Sol raises the bar for coding, biology, and cybersecurity workflows.
  • The preview pairs stronger capability with layered safeguards and automated red-teaming.
  • Initial access runs through the API and Codex for selected trusted partners before broader release.

SOURCE 02
TechRadar · June 25, 2026 · Security

Malicious OpenClaw skills found online

Source 02 · TechRadar

Multiple malicious OpenClaw skills found online

TechRadar reports Unit 42 found five malicious ClawHub skills, including two macOS infostealers, before the listings and accounts were removed.

https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/multiple-malicious-openclaw-skills-found-online-including-two-macos-infostealers

Why it matters
  • Skills can package powerful agent behavior, so marketplace trust matters.
  • Mac users are explicitly in scope through AMOS-style infostealer payloads.
  • Good default behavior: inspect sources, pin trusted skills, and remove anything suspicious.
