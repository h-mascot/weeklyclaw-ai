What this episode covers

  • OpenAI stacked chip, model, harness, and business seat into one vertically aligned agent machine.

  • Headlong made persistence the product while cost, secret boundaries, and self-stop failures remained governance problems.
  • Perplexity moved the agent appliance onto the desk with local-first execution and approval before cloud calls.
  • Figure turned the robot race into a data race: millions of videos, creator payouts, and a planned billion-dollar data and compute spend.
  • The durable advantage is the deployment layer — spend boundaries, permissions, and who decides what the agent does next.
