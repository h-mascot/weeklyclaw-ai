## Grid A — scale, science, and local execution (8:00)

- **Card 1 — Mistral Large 4 (about 2:00).** Mistral reports 1T total parameters and 52B active. Its API preview is available; weights are promised for the end of October, so do not call the weights released. Artificial Analysis lists an index of 38 against a 26 median and unusually high output volume. Attribute the frontier-comparison claim to Mistral.
- **Card 2 — Reflection Beam (about 2:00).** The preview is 501B total / 23B active. Reflection has promised weights, a technical report, and a model card later this month. Its benchmark chart is self-reported; independent comparisons wait for weights.
- **Card 3 — OpenAI math release and the separate Alman–Williams paper (about 2:00).** OpenAI launched 722 manuscripts across 372 result families; three were withdrawn on October 7, leaving 719, and 14 others received substantive repairs. **300 of 719 top-line results are formalized (~42%)**; the repository is Apache-2.0 and the manuscripts are not peer reviewed. Separately, the Alman–Williams paper says Claude discovered the faster algorithm; Josh Alman and Virginia Vassilevska Williams developed and authored the paper. Keep the repository and paper distinct; do not attribute the paper to OpenAI. If mentioning Unitary Synthesis, qualify it as suitable for a Boolean oracle.
- **Card 4 — Microsoft Windows hybrid intelligence (about 2:00).** Execution Containers are generally available on Windows 11 and support OpenClaw. HydraFusion is an experimental preview expected later in October. Copilot local context, actions, and models are planned for coming months on Copilot+ PCs; the Windows OpenClaw gateway is native. Keep GA, preview, and future plans distinct.

## Grid B — agents meet the systems around them (8:00)

- **Card 5 — ThinkingBox (about 2:00).** Microsoft and Hugging Face evaluate whether agents leave the right state behind. In a common-set ablation across 12 models, 79,853 of 121,680 valid trials failed. Keep that benchmark setup attached to the two-thirds figure.
- **Card 6 — Grok Bot and native X actions (about 2:00).** Cursor documents backend routing to Claude Opus 5.5 when appropriate; there is no added per-token charge merely for that Opus routing and no per-user model picker. Separate primary posts say Grok Bot can search, read, and monitor X natively for all users without configuring an X connector. Do not imply every request routes to Claude or make free/unlimited claims.
- **Card 7 — Google Gemini agent (about 2:00).** At Gemini at Work 2026 on October 8, Google announced the Gemini agent: a shared workplace agent with persistent cloud execution, coworker agents that share memory and have dedicated identities, and orchestration across Gemini and Claude. Spend caps can pause work at the limit. Small-business early access is available; broader access is planned. This is an announcement, not a tested launch, and there is no universal price or date to quote.
- **Card 8 — Nous Research (about 2:00).** Nous announced a $90M raise and its intent to build Hermes for Businesses. The business product has not launched; distinguish the plan from the existing Hermes developer project. Treat this as strategic context, not a cause or proof of consumer retreat.

## Grid C — policy, serving security, and naming (4:00)

- **Card 9 — Anthropic usage policy (about 1:20).** The October 8 update takes effect November 12 and prohibits sustained, needless abusive or cruel behavior toward models. It carves out common user frustration and pushback, dark creative themes, and testing or research. Anthropic's announcement describes ending the conversation as the principal enforcement. Do not call this the first such lab policy.
- **Card 10 — LMCache RCE (about 1:20).** JFrog reported an unauthenticated code-execution issue in LMCache's multiprocess ZeroMQ transport on October 7. A reachable network listener can expose pickle deserialization with the LMCache process's privileges; official containers run as root. No fix was available as of October 7. Scope the risk to exposed, reachable deployments; a default localhost bind is not remotely reachable.
- **Card 11 — Peter announces .claw incoming (about 1:20).** On October 8, Peter Steinberger posted, “WE GOT IT! .claw incoming!” and quoted David Rodecker calling the OpenClaw Foundation a winning applicant. ICANN's application still shows **Active Pre-Evaluation Processing**; no final delegation or domain-registration availability is established.
