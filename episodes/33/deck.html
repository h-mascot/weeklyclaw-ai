WHAT HAPPENED THIS WEEK
What happened this week · part 3

A policy update, a cache-layer RCE, and a .claw application.

Three distinct status updates: Anthropic revises its usage policy, a network-exposed LMCache transport has a critical RCE advisory, and ICANN lists an active application for the .claw top-level domain.

Anthropic updates its usage policy on model-directed abuse.

Announced Oct 8, effective Nov 12. The primary announcement says enforcement centers on ending conversations; carve-outs include ordinary frustration, creative themes, and research.

▶ PRIMARY · POLICY UPDATE

LMCache has a critical RCE in its multiprocess transport.

An unauthenticated pickle message can run code with the process’s privileges when the ZeroMQ service is network-exposed. Official containers run as root; no fix was listed as of Oct 7. Default localhost binds are not remotely reachable.

▶ JFROG ADVISORY · CVE-2026-105192

Peter announces “.claw incoming.”

He quoted David Rodecker’s report naming the OpenClaw Foundation as winning applicant. ICANN still lists Active — Pre-Evaluation Processing; final delegation and domain-registration availability are not established.

04 / HOT TAKE · 4 MIN
Hot take · two views, one question

Who wins the consumer agent?

HENRY · OCT 7 THESIS

Polished UX is pulling consumers toward closed agents.

Henry argues that users are leaving open-source harnesses for Grok Bot, Muse, and Dot. He reads OpenClaw’s enterprise emphasis and Hermes’ coding/science focus as a retreat from consumers. That is his interpretation, not measured market-share data.

▶ HENRY’S POST · OCT 7
TEKNIUM · OCT 6 POSITION

Wider-use focus, with consumer mobile plans.

Teknium says consumers are not the only demographic: scientists, cybersecurity, developers, and knowledge workers matter too. He says mobile will be almost exclusively consumer-focused.
