06 / HOT TAKE · 4 MIN
Hot take · the receipts

Anthropic’s revenue is the headline. The commitments are the story.

S-1/A · SEC EDGAR · unaudited, vendor-filed · Q3-26 $16,956M vs $895.4B of purchase commitments, ~13x annualized revenue.

The proposition

Nobody signs $895.4B of compute commitments by accident. That is a demand forecast — and if it is even 20% wrong, the balance sheet that absorbs it is not Anthropic’s.

What would change Henry’s mind: a disclosed commitment schedule where the $895.4B steps up only after revenue clears a stated threshold.

Henry

Thirteen times annualized revenue, signed before the roadshow. If compute demand flattens, the people holding the leases find out first.

Andy

Commitments are how you get priority allocation in a supply-constrained market. The question is not the size, it is whether the revenue curve keeps bending the way Q3 did — +46.7% quarter on quarter, $33.2B across nine months.
