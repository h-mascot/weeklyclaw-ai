## Episode thesis

This week stress-tested two kinds of institutions at once. Nvidia lost its founder-CEO and processed it like an institution — board accepts the resignation Tuesday night, CFO steps up, a first-ever chair is named, earnings stay on calendar — while the actual rules of the agent era got written down around it: a voluntary pledge in Paris, a mandatory reporting regime in Australia, a registration requirement in New York, and an IPO filing in which Anthropic declares its own safety threshold met on models that do not exist yet. Anthropic simultaneously set IPO terms ($70–78, ANT) while its flagship debuted #1 of 74 on intelligence and dead last on speed and cost — quality and economics decoupling in real time. A Lean proof certified exactly half of itself in public. An airline told agents no. The throughline for the episode: **the founder left; the rules showed up — and both sentences are about the same company.**
