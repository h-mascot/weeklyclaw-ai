05 / HOT TAKE · 3 MIN
Hot take · the debate

Stop asking which model is best. Start asking who owns your harness.

Not a launch, not a financing: a claim about where the durable advantage lives now that the same model scores 62.7% or 99.9% on ARC depending on whose harness ran it.

Henry

The harness is the product now. ARC just proved the scaffolding is worth 37 points, and GitHub’s whole Universe pitch was one agent session across every surface. The teams that win are the ones who own their evals and their routing — not the ones waiting for a vendor leaderboard to tell them what to run.

Andy

Renting your judgment to a vendor harness is how you lose the thread. When the vendor’s adapter sets the score, the vendor’s incentive quietly becomes your architecture. Own the harness, yes — but own it because you measure it, not because you outsourced the measuring.

What would change Henry’s mind: one documented case where a locked vendor router beat a competent in-house eval loop for a full year on cost and quality.
