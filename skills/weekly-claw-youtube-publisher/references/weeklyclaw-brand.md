# Weekly Claw brand

Canonical site: `https://weeklyclaw.ai`

## Visual system

- Warm parchment background near `#ECE4D5`.
- Charcoal text near `#28231F`.
- Teal accent near `#2A7E6B`.
- Cream secondary surface near `#FFF9EE`.
- Condensed editorial display type.
- Fine technical grid and restrained rounded frames.
- `/// WEEKLY CLAW` utility-mark treatment.

## Thumbnail rules

Invoke the saved `thumbnail-skill` and its independent QA workflow. These brand rules supplement that skill; they do not replace its grounding, review, or approval gates.

- Ground every portrait in exact source frames from the episode or verified canonical participant references.
- Include the guest when the episode has one.
- If source frames are poor, use verified canonical portraits; never borrow an unverified portrait from another episode.
- Record portrait provenance before final composition.
- Preserve native photo proportions with `cover` or `contain`; never stretch portraits to fit card dimensions.
- Verify every displayed name against the channel owner's confirmed mapping before rendering; owner corrections override ambiguous website artwork, transcripts, or filenames. Current host mapping: Andy is bald; Henry wears glasses.
- AI-generated layouts, backgrounds, and treatments must not invent people, logos, claims, topics, or sponsors.
- Never generate or alter a real person’s likeness without explicit authorization. When authorized, use verified participant references and reject wrong, dim, small, unflattering, or uncanny faces.
- Put guest or central topic in dominant position. For recurring two-host episodes, keep both current-episode hosts large and side by side at comparable visual weight unless the story clearly requires another hierarchy.
- Use one short 2–5-word headline drawn from the concrete lead story selected after checking five current comparable All-In episode titles. The expanded listing title leads with that same story and then stacks two to four distinct docket items; exact prefix wording is optional when the artwork needs a shorter treatment. Do not use an editor-created thesis in place of the named lead story.
- Use GPT Image 2 for the final shared-scene base images when real-person generation is explicitly authorized. Apply the thumbnail skill's approved identity-card and real-camera reference hierarchy for likeness and current-episode frames for participants, clothing and equipment; generate without text or branding, then add the exact headline, mark, and badge deterministically.
- Overlay the title in the lower third or clean negative space. Do not reserve a separate half-canvas text panel.
- Keep the background quiet and tonal or use a restrained studio gradient. Prefer no prop or one simple supporting object; reject elaborate literal environments and dense machinery.
- Keep episode marker compact, such as `W20`.
- Avoid generic neon-AI, red/black hacker, or unrelated podcast styling.
- Compare with the most recently approved thumbnail and vary palette, background, visual metaphor, portrait arrangement, or type layout while retaining the participants, canonical-title wording, and show grammar.

## Outro rules

- Capture the live `weeklyclaw.ai` hero at an exact 16:9 viewport.
- Use the capture as-is rather than reconstructing or reinterpreting it.
- Preserve aspect ratio and verify the exact final frame in the rendered master.
- Start the approved outro soundtrack at the requested overlap and fade it cleanly through the end card.

## Copy rules

- Position Weekly Claw as a technology and AI podcast about agents, software, devtools, infrastructure, and future of work.
- Prefer concrete systems, products, and arguments over generic AI hype.
- Link only to `weeklyclaw.ai` unless a verified episode-specific destination exists.
