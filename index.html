The format

Segments, not slots.

The show follows the excitement. These are the recurring segments — any given week runs the ones the week deserves.

The Wire

The week, fast

What actually happened in AI and agents this week, cut for people who build — no press-release recycling.

Tool Fight

Two tools. One task. Live.

Head-to-head on a real job, on air, with a verdict. Vendors welcome to show up and defend their corner.

Demo

A builder ships on air

Guest builders demo what they're making — agents, devtools, weird workflows — live, with real questions.

Hot Takes

Debates, on record

The arguments everyone is having in DMs, had in public. Positions get taken and clipped.

Postmortem

Agent failures, autopsied

The runaway cron job, the $400 loop, the deleted repo. What broke, why, and what it teaches.

Breaking

News drops, we go live

When something big lands mid-week, the show doesn't wait for Friday. Live reaction while it matters.
